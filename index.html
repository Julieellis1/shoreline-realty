Yes. An agent saves you money and time, and gives you access to more rental properties than you would find on your own.

Do you help tenants and landlords?

Yes, both. We find tenants for landlords and homes for tenants, with the same care either way.

What areas do you cover?

Mississauga (Port Credit, Lakeview, Clarkson, Erindale and Applewood), Oakville, Burlington, Etobicoke, Downtown Toronto and Hamilton.

Will I feel pressured?

No. In one client's words, Marco “never pressured us to make an offer.”

Shoreline Realty Corp., Brokerage is a full-service real estate brokerage helping families buy, sell, and lease homes for over 35 years in Ontario. What started as a boutique style real estate agency in Mississauga, Shoreline Realty has expanded to service multiple cities across Ontario.

Our growth has not stopped us from providing carefully curated home buying and selling strategies for our clients. We recognize everyone's real estate needs are unique and your plan should reflect that. We build life-long relationships with all our clients giving us a strong reputation of excellence within the industry.
