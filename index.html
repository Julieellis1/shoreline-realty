Who we are.

Shoreline Realty Corp., Brokerage is a full-service real estate brokerage helping families buy, sell, and lease homes for over 35 years in Ontario. What started as a boutique style real estate agency in Mississauga, Shoreline Realty has expanded to service multiple cities across Ontario. Our growth has not stopped us from providing carefully curated home buying and selling strategies for our clients. We recognize everyone's real estate needs are unique and your plan should reflect that. We build life-long relationships with all our clients giving us a strong reputation of excellence within the industry. Just like the lighthouse in our logo, we are always here for you.
