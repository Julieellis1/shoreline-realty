Our story

A family brokerage, then and now.

What started as a boutique agency in Mississauga now serves families across Ontario, without losing the personal touch it began with.

Founded by Dagmar and John, Shoreline Realty Corp., Brokerage has been servicing the Lakeview community for more than 25 years. What started as a small, independent brokerage is now a boutique-style firm serving the Greater Toronto Area. Marco Pedri, raised in the business since birth, now continues the legacy as a broker himself.

We build life-long relationships with all our clients, giving us a strong reputation of excellence within the industry. Just like the lighthouse in our logo, we are always here for you.
